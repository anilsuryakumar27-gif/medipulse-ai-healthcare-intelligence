### Part 3: Prompt Engineering & Iterative Refinement
*(Visual: Briefly open or reference `PROMPT_DOCUMENTATION.md` or stay on dashboard)*

> *"A critical part of this project was iterative prompt engineering. In my initial drafts, the AI generated generic sales pitches that sounded like spam and hallucinated US concepts like HIPAA.*
>
> *I refined my prompts through three distinct iterations:*
> - *First, **Domain Grounding & System Personas**: I instructed the AI to act as an Australian Healthcare Operations Consultant, grounding it in RACGP standards and Medicare Chronic Disease Management items 721 and 723.*
> - *Second, **Negative Constraints**: I banned aggressive sales buzzwords and enforced a consultative, peer-to-peer Australian professional tone.*
> - *Third, **Structured Output Formatting**: I enforced strict JSON schemas to reliably feed our front-end modal and CRM export pipeline."*

> *I solved this by combining regex pre-processing with prompt instructions to strip post-nominal degrees and accurately isolate doctor surnames, like 'Dr. Jenkins'.*
